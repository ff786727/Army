PAKISTAN ARMY

History

Pakistan Army 1947 me wajood me aayi. Tab se ab tak hamari Army ne 1965, 1971 ki jangon aur Dushman ke khilaf jung me be-misaal qurbani di hai. Hamari Army dunya ki behtareen professional forces me shumar hoti hai.


Our Mission

Pakistan ki sarhadon ki hifazat, qomi salamti ko yaqeeni banana, aur qaum ki har mushkil me madad karna hamara mission hai.


Core Values

Imaan: Allah par mukammal bharosa
Taqwa: Allah ka khof
Jihad Fi Sabeelillah: Allah ki raah me jihad


Message from COAS

"Pakistan Army hamesha qaum ki umeedon par poora utregi. Ham watan ke har dushman ka muqabla karne ke liye tayyar hain."
