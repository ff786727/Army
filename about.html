PAKISTAN ARMY

History

Founded: 14 August 1947
Country: Pakistan
Pakistan Army 1947 me wajood me aayi. Tab se ab tak hamari Army ne 1965, 1971 ki jangon aur dushman ke khilaf be-misaal qurbani di hai. 560,000 active-duty aur 565,000 reserve personnel ke saath ye duniya ki behtareen professional forces me shumar hoti hai. GHQ: Rawalpindi, Punjab

Our Mission

Pakistan ki sarhadon ki hifazat, qomi salamti ko yaqeeni banana, aur qaum ki har mushkil me madad karna hamara mission hai.
Motto: Iman, Taqwa, Jihad Fi Sabeelillah
Colours: Green and White
Anniversary: Defence Day - 6 September
