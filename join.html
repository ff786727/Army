🎖️ Pakistan Army Soldier Recruitment

Serve the Nation. Live with Honor 🇵🇰

1. General Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 17.5 to 23 Years
  • Marital Status: Unmarried (or Married/Unmarried male citizens as specified for certain roles)
  • Gender: Male
  • Minimum Height: 5'6" (167.5 cm)
  • Weight: As per Body Mass Index (BMI) standards
  • Nationality: Male citizens of Pakistan, Azad Kashmir, and Gilgit-Baltistan

2. Available Positions / Vacancies

Soldier (General Duty - GD)

Technical Soldier

Military Police (MP) Soldier

Soldier Driver

Soldier Clerk

Nursing Assistant Soldier

Cook / Sanitary Worker

Note: All positions offer pension benefits upon completion of service.

3. Required Documents for Registration

  • Educational Certificates: Original + 2 attested photocopies
  • CNIC / Smart Card: Original Computerized National Identity Card
  • Father’s CNIC: Attested photocopy
  • Domicile: Domicile Certificate / State Subject Certificate
  • Photographs: 4 recent passport-size photographs
  • Attestation: All documents attested by Government Gazetted Officer
